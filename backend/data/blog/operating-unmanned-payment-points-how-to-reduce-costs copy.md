In modern urban planning, operating a parking lot is not just about managing a parking area, but also about managing a data and financial operation. In traditionally managed parking lots, the largest expense item is always personnel costs. However, advancing license plate recognition technologies and unmanned payment booths are radically changing this cost structure.

### Financial Advantages of Automation
For a parking lot to operate 24 hours a day, at least 3 shifts of personnel are required. Thanks to unmanned payment points, this cost is directly reduced to zero. License plate recognition technology creates a system record at the moment the vehicle enters. When the driver completes their payment through kiosks or mobile applications before exiting, the barrier opens automatically. The error rate in this process is almost negligible.

### Technical Infrastructure and Efficiency
The parking software used must work in full integration with the hardware (cameras, sensors, barriers). Especially the advancements in image processing technologies provide a high accuracy rate even in bad weather conditions or with dirty plates. This minimizes the need for manual intervention, and thus the need for technical personnel. The reduction in maintenance costs and the capacity for non-stop collection 24/7 significantly shorten the return on investment (ROI) period.
