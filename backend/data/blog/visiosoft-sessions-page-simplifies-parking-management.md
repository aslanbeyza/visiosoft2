Running a parking facility smoothly requires accurate tracking of every vehicle entry and exit. At sites with heavy daily traffic, monitoring all movement from one place makes life easier for staff and keeps operations running in order.

The <strong>Sessions Page</strong> in Visiosoft <a href="/en/parking-software">parking software</a> is designed to show all vehicle activity on a single screen — from active parking sessions to completed ones, payment details to movement records, in a clear and practical layout.

### View All Sessions on One Screen

One of the biggest needs in parking management is fast access to up-to-date information. With the Sessions Page, operators can track vehicles inside the lot, vehicles that have exited, and completed transactions from one view.

During peak hours, staff no longer need to jump between screens. Everything stays in one place for better control. For other operational and financial visibility, see our <a href="/en/parking-software-reports">parking software reports</a> page.

### Find What You Need in Seconds

Daily operations often require locating a specific vehicle or checking payment status. Searching through long record lists wastes time.

The plate search feature on the Visiosoft Sessions Page brings up vehicle information within seconds. Enter a plate number and the relevant session appears with all details ready to review. This reduces staff workload and minimizes waiting time for drivers.

### All Vehicle Information in One Place

Understanding a vehicle's status in the lot does not require multiple screens. The Sessions Page displays plate number, entry and exit times, parking duration, fee, payment status, and session details in a single table.

Managers and operations teams can analyze the current situation easily. This helps prevent information loss during shift changes or busy periods. Speed, control, and accurate data are essential in parking operations. The Visiosoft Sessions Page gives operators a central view of all vehicle movement and a real operational advantage.
