Old-type ticket machines and cumbersome barrier systems are giving way to more compact and intelligent hardware. In a modern parking installation process, the most critical component is a license plate recognition module with a high accuracy rate. This approach reduces hardware costs and also minimizes the need for technical service.

Software that can be updated remotely ensures that your system always runs with the latest features. The elimination of physical card or ticket consumption allows you to demonstrate an environmentally friendly approach and significantly reduces your operating expenses in the long run. Intelligent automation transforms your parking lot into not just a parking area, but a profitable technology hub.
