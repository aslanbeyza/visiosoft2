Today, parking management is no longer limited to simply controlling vehicle entry and exit. Businesses are adopting technology-driven solutions to build more organized systems and prevent time loss. **Visiosoft** unmanned parking systems provide major convenience at this stage.

One of the key features of these systems is the **whitelist**. With the whitelist, vehicles that should not be charged can be defined easily. For example, the business owner’s vehicle, management vehicles, staff vehicles, and other vehicles with free access rights can be added to this list. As a result, these vehicles can enter and exit the parking area without any charge.

This feature brings significant comfort, especially in high-traffic operations. Instead of handling manual actions every time, the plate is registered once and the process continues automatically. This approach saves time and prevents operational confusion.

Another major benefit is structured record tracking. You can view all whitelisted vehicles directly in the panel or export them as an Excel file from the panel. This allows businesses to check, update, and archive the list whenever needed.

All of these operations are enabled by an advanced **license plate recognition system**. When a vehicle enters, the plate is read automatically, checked against system records, and authorized vehicles receive fast access. This reduces human error and speeds up operations.

In short, the whitelist feature in parking management delivers both convenience and a more professional operation model. With **Visiosoft** unmanned parking solutions, parking workflows become more practical, faster, and more controlled.
