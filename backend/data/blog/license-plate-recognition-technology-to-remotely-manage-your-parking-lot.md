The era of physically standing at the head of the parking lot has come to an end. With an advanced license plate recognition system and cloud-based infrastructure, you can manage operations from anywhere in the world. Monitoring vehicle entry and exit in real-time, and remotely controlling barriers in cases requiring manual intervention, are now standard features.

These systems, which alleviate the administrative burden of operating a parking lot, provide transparent reporting. All data, such as the number of vehicles that entered, the duration of their stay, and the revenue generated, are updated in real-time on your management panel. Full control and location-independent management are the keys to modern operations.
