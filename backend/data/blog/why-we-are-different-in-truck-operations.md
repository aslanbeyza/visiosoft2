When it comes to logistics and parking management, standard solutions are often not enough to meet real operational needs in the field. At visiosoft, we take a different perspective and offer a model that delivers real operational advantages, especially for truck operations.

## Traditional Approach vs visiosoft Approach

Many parking and facility management systems treat the truck and trailer as if they were a single vehicle and apply pricing accordingly. In reality, field operations are much more dynamic.

Consider a simple scenario:

- A truck with a trailer enters your parking facility.
- The truck's only task is to drop off the trailer.
- Later, another truck arrives and picks up that same trailer.

In traditional systems, this process can result in:

- A fee charged to the first truck.
- Another fee charged to the second truck that picks up the trailer.

This effectively means charging twice for the same load.

## What We Do

At visiosoft, we handle this in a much fairer and operationally accurate way:

- The truck is only the carrier.
- The real value and parking duration belong to the trailer.

For this reason, we charge based on the trailer, not the truck.

## Advantages for Your Operation

### 1) Fair Pricing

It prevents charging multiple vehicles for the same load. Your customers avoid unnecessary costs.

### 2) Operational Flexibility

Even if trucks change, your system remains stable. Since the trailer is the constant asset, tracking and management become much easier.

### 3) Transparent Process Management

It is clear who pays for what. Disputes and misunderstandings are significantly reduced.

### 4) Alignment with Real Logistics Operations

Your system reflects what actually happens in the field. This improves both operational efficiency and trust.

## Conclusion

Treating trucks and trailers as separate entities may look like a minor detail, but it makes a major difference in operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.
