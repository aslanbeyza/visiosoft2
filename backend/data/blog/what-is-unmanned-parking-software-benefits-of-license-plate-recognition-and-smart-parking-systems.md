As urbanization accelerates worldwide, parking management has become a critical operational requirement for businesses. While traditional parking methods bring challenges such as human error, time loss, and high staffing costs, new-generation unmanned parking software offers modern solutions to these problems.

Smart parking software solutions developed by Visiosoft provide businesses with a fully digital management infrastructure through license plate recognition, HGS parking integration, and kiosk payment systems.

### What Is an Unmanned Parking System?

An unmanned parking system is an intelligent solution where vehicle entry and exit are controlled automatically, payments are processed digitally, and all operations are managed through centralized software.

In these systems:

- License plate recognition cameras
- HGS integration
- Parking automation software
- Kiosk payment systems
- Central management panel

work together to deliver a fast, secure, and efficient parking experience.

### How Does License Plate Recognition Work?

License plate recognition (LPR) automatically detects and records vehicle plates through cameras. Thanks to this technology, entry and exit operations are completed within seconds.

With license plate recognition:

- Vehicle congestion is reduced
- Manual control is eliminated
- Security levels are increased
- Revenue leakage is prevented
- Parking management becomes easier

LPR systems have become standard especially in shopping malls, residential sites, hospitals, universities, and commercial parking facilities.

### Contactless Access with HGS Parking Systems

HGS parking systems enable fast and contactless payment processing. Drivers can pass through without cash payments.

With Visiosoft HGS integration:

- Waiting times are reduced
- Vehicle traffic flows faster
- User satisfaction increases
- An automated payment infrastructure is established

This system provides significant advantages especially for businesses with high vehicle entry volumes.

### Fast Collection with Kiosk Payment Systems

In modern parking facilities, kiosk payment systems significantly improve the user experience. Drivers can complete payments quickly without staff assistance.

With kiosk software:

- Cash and card payments are accepted
- Queue times are reduced
- 24/7 payment is available
- Staffing costs are lowered

Visiosoft kiosk solutions work in full integration with parking automation systems to ensure uninterrupted operations.
